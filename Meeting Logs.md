## Meeting two
### 10/28/2026

During this meeting, we began to shift from the general plan to a tangible design.
We decided that it would make more sense to focus on one electrostatic cleaning
method than two distinct ones, in order to focus more on the quality of the 
device and app. We settled on two servo motors which permit a full hemisphere of
motion to face the sun at any given point. Also, we decided to use a raspberry
pi pico microcontroller to control the system. We furthered our BOM sheet with
the relevant materials and prices
